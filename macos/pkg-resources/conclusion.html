Installed

Plug in your NS7 and switch it on. Within a few seconds "Numark NS7" appears in your audio settings and DJ app.

If macOS asks whether to allow the accessory to connect, click Allow.

To remove the driver, run in Terminal:
/usr/local/share/ns7driver/uninstall.sh

Logs: /Library/Logs/ns7d.log and /tmp/ns7-midi.log
